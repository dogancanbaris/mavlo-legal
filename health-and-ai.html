Health data and AI

Effective date: [publication date] · How Mavlo handles your health information and uses AI. This supplements the Privacy Policy.

Your health information

  • Mavlo keeps what you log and tell it (food and drinks, activity, weight, goals, allergies, injuries and similar details) so it can help you. You approve lasting details on a card before Mavlo remembers them, and you can ask Mavlo to change or forget any of them.
  • With your permission, Mavlo reads four kinds of Apple Health data: steps, workouts, active energy and body weight. You can change this in the Health app at any time.
  • Sensitive details, such as mental, reproductive or sexual health, are used only after you agree to that category in the app.
  • We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never use Apple Health data for marketing or data mining.

How Mavlo uses AI

  • Mavlo uses Google Gemini, through Google's paid API, to understand your messages, photos, videos and voice notes and to write replies. Before any of your information is sent, the app asks for your permission. If you decline or later withdraw it, Mavlo cannot answer with AI, but your saved records stay yours.
  • What is sent: your message, and only the context needed to answer it, such as your recent conversation, today's totals and the details you approved. It is sent when you send a message.
  • Google does not use prompts or responses sent through the paid API to improve its products. Google keeps them for a limited period solely to detect and prevent abuse, under the Gemini API terms. Google must protect this information to a standard at least equal to ours.
  • AI can be wrong. Mavlo shows the source for health answers, says when it has none, and asks you to confirm anything it saves. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe.

Your choices

  • Withdraw AI sharing, export your data or delete your account in Account settings.
  • Ask Mavlo to forget something, or open a remembered item and remove it.
