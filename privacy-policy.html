Mavlo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 June 2026 · Last updated: 2 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dogancan Baris ("we", "us"), the individual developer of the Mavlo mobile app ("Mavlo", "the app"), collects, uses, shares, and protects your information. Mavlo is an AI health and diet coach that lets you log food, workouts, sleep, and weight by chatting, typing, speaking, or taking a photo. Questions: barisdogancan@gmail.com.

In short: We collect the information you give Mavlo to run your account and coach you. Your inputs may be processed by trusted AI providers to generate responses. We do not sell your data and we never use your health or nutrition data for advertising. You can export or permanently delete your data at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account & profile: email, display name, and (if you choose social sign-in) the identifier from Apple or Google; plus profile basics, preferences, language, units, and your assistant settings.
  • Health & fitness data you log: body metrics (height, weight, age range, and sex/gender if you provide it), goals, diet and fasting preferences, foods and recipes, calories and macros, workouts and activity, and notes.
  • Photos: images of meals, nutrition labels, menus, or routines that you choose to capture or upload for analysis.
  • Voice: short voice recordings and their transcripts when you use voice input.
  • Conversations & memory: the messages you exchange with Mavlo and the preferences/facts you approve Mavlo to remember.

Information collected with your permission

  • Device health data: with your explicit permission, data from Apple Health / Health Connect such as steps, workouts, active energy, body weight, sleep, and recovery. You can decline and still use Mavlo manually.
  • Purchases: your subscription/entitlement status (via RevenueCat and the App Store / Google Play). We do not receive your full card details.
  • Device & diagnostic data: app version, device/OS, and de-identified error, performance, and usage events used to keep Mavlo reliable.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and personalize Mavlo's coaching, logging, estimates, and reports.
  • To remember your preferences so you don't have to repeat yourself.
  • To keep Mavlo safe and reliable (abuse prevention, debugging, support).
  • To process your subscription and manage your account.

We process this data to deliver features you request and to operate your account. We do not use it to make automated decisions with legal effects.

3. How your information is shared

We share information only with service providers that help us run Mavlo, and only as needed to deliver a feature you use. These providers act on our behalf and are restricted from using your data for their own purposes.

  • AI providers (e.g., Google Gemini, OpenAI): your messages, and the food/label photos and voice you choose to share, may be processed to generate responses, estimates, and transcriptions.
  • Nutrition data sources (e.g., USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts): used to look up foods and barcodes.
  • Billing (RevenueCat, Apple, Google): to process and validate your subscription.
  • Analytics & reliability: de-identified diagnostics to keep the app working.
We never sell your personal data. We never use your health, fitness, or nutrition data for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we never share it with data brokers. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Permissions

Mavlo asks for permissions only when a feature needs them, with an in-app explanation: Camera (meal/label photos), Photo Library (choosing photos), Microphone (voice check-ins), Health (optional device health import), and Notifications (reminders). You can change or revoke these in your device settings at any time.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data while your account exists. Raw food photos and raw voice audio are processed transiently and are not retained by default; the entries and transcripts you keep remain until you edit or delete them. When you delete your account, we erase your Mavlo-owned data as described below.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access & export: request a copy of your data.
  • Delete: permanently delete your account and data in-app (Settings → Delete account) or via our account deletion page.
  • Withdraw consent: revoke permissions (camera, photos, microphone, health, notifications) in device settings, or disable individual memories in-app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, portability, objection). Contact us to exercise them.

7. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app, or by following our account deletion instructions. Deletion is immediate and permanent for your Mavlo-owned data. Some records may be retained where required (for example, billing/transaction records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat, or where retention is legally required).

8. Security

We protect data in transit with encryption (HTTPS/TLS), restrict access to authenticated requests, scope data to its owner, and avoid logging sensitive content. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

Mavlo is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Wellness disclaimer

Mavlo is a wellness and fitness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any disease, and its estimates (such as calories) are approximate and shown with their source and a confidence level. Always consult a qualified professional for medical, dietary, or health decisions.

11. International users

Mavlo is available globally. Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live, including by the service providers listed above. We take steps to protect your information consistent with this policy wherever it is processed.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice. Continued use of Mavlo after an update means you accept the revised policy.
