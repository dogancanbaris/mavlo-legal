Delete your Mavlo account & data

App: Mavlo: AI Health & Diet Coach · Developer: Dogancan Baris

You can permanently delete your Mavlo account and all of your Mavlo-owned data at any time. There are two ways:

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mavlo and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm by typing DELETE when prompted.

Your account and data are erased immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll complete the deletion for you.

Request deletion by email
📧 barisdogancan@gmail.com — email deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
This cannot be undone. Consider exporting anything you want to keep before deleting.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile, and preferences.
  • Your food, recipe, workout, weight, sleep, and fasting logs.
  • Your conversations and the preferences Mavlo remembered about you.
  • Photos and voice content associated with your account.
  • Imported device health data held by Mavlo.
